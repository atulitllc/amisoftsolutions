The company

We build the platforms regulated science can qualify.

AmiSoft Solutions builds GxP-ready computing environments, clinical and R&D data platforms, and analytics workspaces for pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and medical device organizations. We make the stack something a quality unit can inspect — and science and IT can operate.

What we do

Product and services for governed scientific work.

Life sciences organizations need computing and data platforms that survive a quality review without becoming a black box. AmiSoft supplies the environments, the integration patterns, and the evidence artifacts delivery teams adapt to the organization’s quality system.

We show up when the architecture, the estimate, or the qualification evidence needs an owner.

We will not describe a platform as validated, certified, or approved. Qualification is the regulated company’s act.

What we build

  • Data platforms & analytics workspaces Governed zones, attributable access, and analysis that stays on the source of record.
  • Scientific computing environments Declared runtimes, package provenance, and a formal path kept apart from exploration.
  • Software & integration services File- and API-based exchange a project plan can staff and hand over.
  • Compliance & qualification support Traceability and test-evidence patterns. Delivery guidance, not a sign-off.
