The company

Behind the engagement. Not in front of the client.

AmiSoft Solutions builds GxP-ready computing environments, clinical and R&D data platforms, and analytics workspaces for firms that implement in life sciences. The consultancy or integrator delivers the engagement. We make the stack something a quality unit can inspect.

The role we take

A product and services partner, under your name.

The firm that already sits with the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, or medical device account keeps the contract. AmiSoft supplies the environments, the integration patterns, and the evidence a delivery team can adapt to the client’s quality system.

We show up when the architecture, the estimate, or the qualification evidence needs an owner.

We do not ask to be introduced as a competing consultancy, and we do not open a parallel motion on the account.

What a partner can implement

  • Data platforms & analytics workspaces Governed zones, attributable access, and analysis that stays on the source of record.
  • Scientific computing environments Declared runtimes, package provenance, and a formal path kept apart from exploration.
  • Software & integration services File- and API-based exchange a project plan can staff and hand over.
  • Compliance & qualification support Traceability and test-evidence patterns. Delivery guidance, not a sign-off.
