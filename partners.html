Strategic partnership

How we work with a strategic partner.

AmiSoft is not building a broad partner network. We work with a single strategic partner on enablement, joint scoping, and implementation support — so the platforms we build can be delivered cleanly into regulated life sciences programs.

Clear roles. One relationship.

01— Enablement

Describe the platform without inventing it in the room.

Regulated buyers punish improvisation. Enablement gives the partner team a narrative, an architecture, and answers that stay inside what AmiSoft will actually implement.

  • A half-page architecture briefing for the people who will be in the room
  • A demo storyline for a clinical or R&D analytics scenario
  • Notes for the questions quality, security, and IT actually ask
  • A one-pager and slides the partner can place under the engagement brand

02— Joint scoping

The proposal should match the system that will be stood up.

Joint scoping is shared architecture work before the estimate is locked — not a second sales team, and not a multi-firm co-sell motion. We join where the technical promise has to match what implementation can deliver.

  • Scoping sessions that separate platform scope from services scope
  • Assumptions we record so a proposal can be defended
  • Proposal language a quality reader will not have to unwind
  • Optional presence when the client asks who stands behind the platform

03— Implementation support

A kit the delivery team can adapt, not a black-box install.

Delivery stays in the partner’s statement of work where that is the model. The kit is the part that statement sometimes leaves thin: architecture, configuration, and the evidence pattern the quality unit will ask to see.

  • A reference architecture for a controlled scientific workspace
  • Configuration guidance for access, packages, and promotion paths
  • Qualification worksheets: requirements, traceability, and test evidence
  • A runbook for change control when the environment is updated

Responsibility

Clear ownership between partner and AmiSoft.

The partner holds

  • The client relationship and commercial lead where applicable
  • Program management and the stakeholder map
  • Process design, change, and training
  • Fit to the client’s quality management system

AmiSoft holds

  • Computing and data-platform design
  • Integration patterns and build support
  • Environment configuration guidance
  • Qualification evidence the delivery team can adapt

The line we keep

Three commitments that keep the relationship clean.

No parallel motion

Where the partner leads the account, we do not open a competing sales path into that account.

No false status

We will not describe the platform as validated, certified, or approved. Qualification is the client’s act.

No hopeful scope

If a pursuit sits outside what we can implement, we say so before it is written into a proposal.
